About John

John Smith is a nationally touring stand-up comedian and writer known for his sharp observational humor and quick crowd work. He has been featured on Comedy Central, Netflix Is A Joke, and hosts the hit weekly podcast "Smithereens."

When he isn't on the road, you can catch him performing regularly at his home clubs in New York City.
